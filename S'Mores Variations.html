S'Mores Variations

S'mores Stuffed Cookies

Ingredients:

  • 1 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • ½ cup unsalted butter, softened
  • ½ cup granulated sugar
  • ½ cup packed brown sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup chocolate chips
  • 1 cup mini marshmallows
  • Graham crackers, broken into small pieces

Method:

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. In a mixing bowl, whisk together the flour and baking soda.
  3. In another bowl, cream together the softened butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar until light and fluffy.
  4. Add the egg and vanilla extract to the butter-sugar mixture and beat until well combined.
  5. Gradually add the flour mixture to the wet ingredients and mix until a dough forms.
  6. Fold in the chocolate chips, mini marshmallows, and broken graham crackers into the dough.
  7. Take a tablespoon-sized portion of dough and flatten it in your palm. Place a piece of chocolate in the center and wrap the dough around it, forming a ball.
  8. Place the dough balls on the prepared baking sheet, leaving space between each one.
  9. Bake for 10-12 minutes or until the cookies are golden brown.
  10. Remove from the oven and let them cool on the baking sheet for a few minutes before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely.

Tips:

  • Be sure the marshmallows and chocolate are well enclosed within the dough to prevent excessive melting and spreading while baking.
  • Adjust the amount of marshmallows and chocolate to your preference.

S'mores Dip

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup chocolate chips or chopped chocolate
  • 1 cup mini marshmallows
  • Graham crackers for dipping

Method:

  • Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
  • Spread the chocolate chips or chopped chocolate in an oven-safe skillet or baking dish.
  • Top the chocolate with a layer of mini marshmallows.
  • Place the skillet or baking dish in the oven and bake for 5-7 minutes or until the marshmallows are golden brown and gooey.
  • Remove from the oven and let it cool for a few minutes before serving.
  • Serve the s'mores dip with graham crackers for dipping.

    • Tips:

    • Keep an eye on the dip while it's in the oven as marshmallows can quickly go from golden brown to burnt.
    • You can add extra toppings like chopped nuts or caramel drizzle for added flavor.
