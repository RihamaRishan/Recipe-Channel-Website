Adaptogenic Lattes

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup milk (dairy or non-dairy)
  • 1 teaspoon adaptogen powder (ashwagandha, reishi, maca, etc.)
  • Spices (cinnamon, nutmeg, etc.)
  • Sweetener (honey, maple syrup, etc.)

Method:

  1. Heat the milk in a saucepan over medium heat.
  2. Add adaptogen powder and spices.
  3. Whisk continuously until hot but not boiling.
  4. Remove from heat, add sweetener to taste, and stir well.
  5. Pour into a mug and enjoy your adaptogenic latte!

Tips:

  • Start with a small amount of adaptogen powder and adjust based on your tolerance and desired effects.
  • Experiment with different spices to enhance the flavor profile.
Enjoy these delightful Adaptogenic Lattes
