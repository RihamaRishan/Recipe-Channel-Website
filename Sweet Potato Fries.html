Sweet Potato Fries

Ingredients:

  • 2 large sweet potatoes, peeled and cut into fries
  • 2 tablespoons cornstarch or arrowroot powder
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Method:

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. In a bowl, toss sweet potato fries with cornstarch or arrowroot powder until evenly coated.
  3. Add olive oil, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper to the bowl. Toss until the fries are well coated.
  4. Spread the fries in a single layer on the baking sheet.
  5. Bake for 20-25 minutes, flipping halfway through, until fries are crispy and golden.

Tips:

  • Ensure the fries are evenly coated with the seasoning for balanced flavor.
  • Use a spatula to flip the fries halfway through baking to ensure even cooking.
Enjoy your crispy Sweet Potato Fries!
