Hibiscus Iced Tea

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups dried hibiscus flowers
  • 6 cups water
  • Sweetener (honey, agave, or sugar)
  • Citrus slices (lemon or orange), for garnish (optional)
  • Ice cubes

Method:

  1. In a saucepan, bring water to a boil.
  2. Add dried hibiscus flowers and let it simmer for 10-15 minutes.
  3. Remove from heat and strain the liquid into a pitcher.
  4. Add sweetener to taste and stir until dissolved.
  5. Refrigerate until cold.
  6. Serve over ice, garnished with citrus slices if desired.
  7. Enjoy your refreshing hibiscus iced tea!

Tips:

  • Adjust the sweetness according to your preference.
  • Feel free to add other flavors like cinnamon sticks or ginger for extra depth.
