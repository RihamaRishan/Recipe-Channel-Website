Zuchini Fritters

Ingredients:

  • 2 medium zucchinis, grated and excess moisture squeezed out
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Olive oil for frying

Method:

  1. Grate the zucchinis using a box grater and squeeze out excess moisture using a clean kitchen towel or paper towels.
  2. In a bowl, combine the grated zucchini, Parmesan cheese, flour, breadcrumbs, beaten egg, minced garlic, parsley, salt, and pepper. Mix well.
  3. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
  4. Scoop a tablespoon of the zucchini mixture and form into a patty. Place it gently in the skillet. Repeat to make more fritters, leaving space between them in the skillet.
  5. Cook the fritters for about 3-4 minutes on each side until golden brown and crispy.
  6. Remove from the skillet and place on a paper towel-lined plate to absorb excess oil.

Tips:

  • Ensure the grated zucchini is well-drained to prevent the fritters from becoming soggy.
  • Use a non-stick skillet and enough oil to coat the bottom for crispier fritters.
  • Serve the fritters with a side of yogurt-based dipping sauce or sour cream mixed with herbs for added flavor.
Enjoy your flavorful Zucchini Fritters!
