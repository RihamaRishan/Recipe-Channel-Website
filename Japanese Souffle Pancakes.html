Japanese Souffle Pancakes

Ingredients:

  • 3 large eggs, separated
  • ¼ cup (60ml) milk
  • ¼ teaspoon vanilla extract
  • ¼ cup (30g) cake flour
  • ½ teaspoon baking powder
  • 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
  • Butter for cooking

Method:

  1. Separate egg whites and yolks into two separate bowls.
  2. In the bowl with egg yolks, add milk and vanilla extract. Mix until combined.
  3. Sift cake flour and baking powder into the yolk mixture. Mix well until smooth.
  4. In the bowl with egg whites, whip until foamy, then gradually add sugar. Whip until stiff peaks form.
  5. Gently fold the egg yolk mixture into the egg whites until just combined.
  6. Heat a non-stick pan over low heat and brush with butter.
  7. Spoon the pancake batter onto the pan (about ¼ cup per pancake), cover, and cook for about 3-4 minutes.
  8. Carefully flip the pancakes and cook for an additional 3-4 minutes until both sides are golden brown.
  9. Serve immediately with desired toppings.

Tips:

  • Use a ring mold to help the pancakes keep their fluffy shape while cooking.
  • Be gentle when folding the egg yolk mixture into the whites to retain the fluffiness.
