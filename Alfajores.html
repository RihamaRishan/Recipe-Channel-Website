Alfajores

Ingredients:

For the cookies:

  • 1 cup cornstarch
  • 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large egg yolks
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Dulce de leche for filling

For dusting:

  • Powdered sugar

Instructions:

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. In a bowl, sift together the cornstarch, flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. Set aside.
  3. In another bowl, cream together the softened butter and sugar until light and fluffy.
  4. Add the egg yolks one at a time, mixing well after each addition. Then stir in the vanilla extract.
  5. Gradually add the dry ingredients into the wet mixture until a soft dough forms.
  6. Roll out the dough on a floured surface to about 1/4-inch thickness. Use a cookie cutter to cut out rounds
    7. .
  7. Place the rounds onto the prepared baking sheet and bake for 10-12 minutes or until the edges are lightly golden.
  8. Remove from the oven and let the cookies cool completely.

Assembly:

  1. Take one cookie and spread a generous amount of dulce de leche on the flat side.
  2. Top it with another cookie to create a sandwich. Repeat with the remaining cookies.
  3. Dust the assembled alfajores with powdered sugar.

Tips:

  • Ensure the butter is softened but not melted for the perfect cookie texture.
  • You can adjust the amount of dulce de leche to suit your preference for filling thickness.
  • Alfajores taste even better after a day or two as the flavors meld together.
Enjoy these delightful Alfajores filled with creamy dulce de leche!
