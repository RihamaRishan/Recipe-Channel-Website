Fruit and Herb-Infused Water

Ingredients:

  • Water
  • Sliced fruits (cucumber, lemon, lime, strawberries, etc.)
  • Fresh herbs (mint, basil, rosemary, etc.)
  • Ice cubes

Method:

  1. Fill a pitcher with water.
  2. Add sliced fruits and fresh herbs.
  3. Refrigerate for at least 1-2 hours to let the flavors infuse.
  4. Serve over ice and enjoy the infused water!

Tips:

  • Adjust the ratio of fruits and herbs to achieve your desired flavor intensity.
  • Prepare in advance for a refreshing, hydrating drink throughout the day.
