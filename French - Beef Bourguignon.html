French - Beef Bourguignon

Ingredients:

  • Stew beef chunks
  • Red wine (Burgundy or Pinot Noir)
  • Carrots, onions, and mushrooms
  • Beef broth
  • Tomato paste
  • Garlic, thyme, and bay leaves
  • Butter or oil for sautéing

    • Method:

    *Preparation Steps:

    • Marinate beef chunks with red wine, garlic, thyme, and bay leaves for a few hours or overnight.

    Cooking Instructions:

    • Sauté marinated beef in a pot until browned, then set aside.
    • Sauté vegetables until soft, then add tomato paste.
    • Return beef to the pot, add beef broth, and simmer until beef is tender.

    Tips:

    • Use a good-quality wine for rich flavor.
    • Slow-cook for a more tender and flavorful dish.
    Enjoy your Beef Bourguignon
