Vegan Chocolate Avocado Mousse

Ingredients:

  • 2 ripe avocados
  • ½ cup cocoa powder
  • ½ cup maple syrup or agave nectar
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt
  • Optional toppings: fresh berries, chopped nuts, shredded coconut

Method:

  1. Scoop the flesh of the avocados into a food processor or blender.
  2. Add cocoa powder, maple syrup or agave nectar, vanilla extract, and a pinch of salt.
  3. Blend the ingredients until smooth and creamy, scraping down the sides as needed.
  4. Taste and adjust sweetness if necessary by adding more maple syrup or agave.
  5. Spoon the mousse into serving bowls or glasses.
  6. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes to chill before serving.
  7. Garnish with fresh berries, chopped nuts, or shredded coconut if desired.

Tips:

  • Ensure the avocados are ripe for a smoother texture and better flavor.
  • Adjust the sweetness to your liking by adding more or less maple syrup/agave.

Vegan Raspberry Coconut Popsicles

Ingredients:

  • 1 ½ cups fresh or frozen raspberries
  • 1 can (14 oz) coconut milk
  • ¼ cup maple syrup or agave nectar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Popsicle molds and sticks

Method:

  1. In a blender, combine raspberries, coconut milk, maple syrup or agave, and vanilla extract.
  2. Blend until smooth and well combined.
  3. Taste the mixture and adjust sweetness if needed.
  4. Pour the raspberry coconut mixture into popsicle molds.
  5. Insert popsicle sticks into the molds.
  6. Freeze for at least 4-6 hours until completely set.

Tips:

  • You can add a few whole raspberries into the molds before pouring the mixture for added texture.
  • To easily remove the popsicles from the molds, run the molds under warm water for a few seconds.
These vegan desserts are simple to prepare and offer a delightful and guilt-free indulgence!
