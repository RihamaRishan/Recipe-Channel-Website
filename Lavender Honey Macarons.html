Lavender Honey Macarons

Ingredients:

For the macaron shells:

  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 3/4 cup almond flour
  • 2 large egg whites, at room temperature
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • Purple gel food coloring (optional)

For the filling:

  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 2 tablespoons honey
  • 1 teaspoon culinary lavender, finely chopped or ground

Instructions:

For the macaron shells

  1. Prepare two baking sheets with parchment paper or silicone mats. Set aside.
  2. In a food processor, pulse together the powdered sugar and almond flour until well combined. Sift the mixture into a large bowl.
  3. In a separate bowl, beat the egg whites until they form soft peaks. Gradually add granulated sugar and continue beating until glossy stiff peaks form. Add a few drops of purple food coloring if desired and mix until evenly colored.
  4. Gently fold the almond flour mixture into the egg whites in three batches until the batter is smooth and falls off the spatula in a ribbon-like consistency.
  5. Transfer the batter into a piping bag fitted with a round tip. Pipe small circles onto the prepared baking sheets, leaving space between each macaron shell.
  6. Tap the baking sheets firmly against the counter to release any air bubbles. Let the macarons sit at room temperature for about 30 minutes until a skin forms on top.
  7. Preheat the oven to 300°F (150°C). Bake the macarons, one sheet at a time, for 15-18 minutes, rotating the sheet halfway through baking.
  8. Once done, remove from the oven and let them cool completely on the baking sheet before carefully removing them.

For the filling:

  1. In a bowl, cream together softened butter, honey, and chopped lavender until smooth.
  2. Match similar-sized macaron shells together and pipe or spread a small amount of the lavender honey filling on one shell. Top it with another shell to create a sandwich.
  3. Refrigerate the assembled macarons for at least 24 hours to allow the flavors to meld together.

Tips:

  • Be precise with measurements and follow the macaronage (folding) technique to achieve the right consistency.
  • Ensure the macarons have formed a skin before baking to get the characteristic "feet" and smooth tops.
Enjoy these delicate and fragrant lavender honey macarons!
