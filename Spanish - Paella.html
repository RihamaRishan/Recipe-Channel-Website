Spanish - Paella

Ingredients:

  • Arborio rice
  • Chicken, shrimp, and chorizo
  • Bell peppers, onions, and peas
  • Saffron threads
  • Chicken broth
  • Olive oil
  • Paprika and garlic powder

Method:

Preparation Steps:

  • Sauté chicken, shrimp, and chorizo in a paella pan, then set aside.

Cooking Instructions:

  • Sauté vegetables in the same pan, then add rice and spices, stirring for a minute.
  • Pour in chicken broth and saffron, then add back the cooked meats.
    • Simmer until rice is cooked and liquid is absorbed.

Tips:

  • Use a wide, shallow pan for even cooking.
  • Let the paella sit for a few minutes before serving for the best flavor.
Enjoy your Paella
