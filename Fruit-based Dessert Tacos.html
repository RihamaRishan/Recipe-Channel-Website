Fruit-based Dessert Tacos

Ingredients:

For the Taco Shells:

  • 6 small flour tortillas
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter or coconut oil
  • 2 tablespoons granulated sugar mixed with 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon

For the Fruit Filling:

  • Assorted fruits (such as strawberries, kiwi, mango, pineapple, etc.), diced
  • 2 tablespoons honey or maple syrup
  • Fresh mint leaves (optional, for garnish)
  • Greek yogurt or whipped cream (optional, for topping)

Method:

For the Taco Shells:

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Lightly brush both sides of the tortillas with melted butter or coconut oil.
  3. Sprinkle both sides of the tortillas with the cinnamon-sugar mixture.
  4. Fold the tortillas in half and gently press them to form taco shell shapes.
  5. Place each folded tortilla shell in between the oven grates to hold its shape while baking. Alternatively, use a taco rack or mold.
  6. Bake in the preheated oven for 10-12 minutes until the shells are crispy and golden brown.
  7. Remove from the oven and let them cool completely.

For the Fruit Filling:

  1. In a mixing bowl, combine the diced fruits.
  2. Drizzle honey or maple syrup over the fruits and gently toss until evenly coated.

Assembly:

  1. Once the taco shells have cooled, fill each shell with the mixed fruits.
  2. Optionally, top the fruit-filled tacos with a dollop of Greek yogurt or whipped cream.
  3. Garnish with fresh mint leaves for an extra touch of freshness.
  4. Serve immediately and enjoy your refreshing fruit dessert tacos!

Tips:

  • Experiment with various combinations of fruits to suit your taste preferences.
  • You can also add a sprinkle of shredded coconut or chopped nuts for extra texture and flavor.
  • Ensure the taco shells are completely cooled before filling them to prevent sogginess.
