Basque Burnt Cheese Cake

Ingredients:

  • 2 lbs (900g) cream cheese, softened
  • 1 ½ cups (300g) granulated sugar
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 ½ cups (360ml) heavy cream
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • ¼ cup (30g) all-purpose flour
  • Parchment paper
  • 9-inch springform pan

Method:

  1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Line the springform pan with parchment paper, ensuring it extends over the sides.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, beat the cream cheese and sugar until smooth and creamy.
  3. Add eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition.
  4. Pour in the heavy cream and vanilla extract, mix until combined.
  5. Gradually add the flour and mix until the batter is smooth.
  6. Pour the batter into the prepared pan.
  7. Bake for 50-60 minutes until the top is dark brown and the center slightly jiggles.
  8. Let it cool at room temperature, then refrigerate for at least 4 hours before serving.

Tips:

  • Use parchment paper to easily lift the cheesecake from the pan after it's chilled.
  • Allow the cheesecake to cool completely before refrigerating for the best texture.
