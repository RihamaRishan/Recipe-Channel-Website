Japanese - Chicken Teriyaki

Ingredients:

  • Chicken thighs or breasts
  • Soy sauce
  • Mirin
  • Sake (or rice vinegar)
  • Brown sugar
  • Garlic and ginger, minced
  • Sesame seeds and green onions for garnish

Method:

Preparation Steps:

  • Marinate chicken in soy sauce, mirin, sake, brown sugar, garlic, and ginger.

Cooking Instructions:

  • Pan-sear or grill chicken until cooked through.
  • Simmer the marinade until thickened, then pour over the cooked chicken.
  • Garnish with sesame seeds and chopped green onions.

Tips:

  • Marinate for at least 30 minutes for better flavor.
  • Baste the chicken with teriyaki sauce while cooking for extra flavor.
