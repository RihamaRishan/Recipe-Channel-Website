Gourmet Caramel Apples

Ingredients:

  • 4-6 medium-sized apples (Granny Smith or any firm variety)
  • 1 package (about 14 ounces) soft caramels
  • 2 tablespoons heavy cream or milk
  • Assorted toppings (chopped nuts, mini chocolate chips, crushed cookies, sprinkles, etc.)
  • Wooden sticks or popsicle sticks for handling

Instructions:

  1. Wash and dry the apples thoroughly. Remove any stems and insert wooden sticks into the stem end of each apple. Place them on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
  2. Unwrap the caramels and place them in a microwave-safe bowl with the heavy cream or milk.
  3. Microwave the caramels and cream in 30-second intervals, stirring in between, until the mixture is smooth and fully melted. Be careful as it can get very hot.
  4. Dip each apple into the melted caramel, swirling and rotating to coat the entire surface. Allow any excess caramel to drip off.
  5. Hold the coated apple over the bowl briefly to let the excess caramel drip off, then place it back onto the parchment-lined baking sheet.
  6. Quickly sprinkle your desired toppings onto the caramel before it sets. Roll the caramel-coated apples in chopped nuts, mini chocolate chips, crushed cookies, sprinkles, or any toppings you prefer.
  7. Allow the caramel apples to cool and set completely at room temperature or in the refrigerator for about 30 minutes before serving.

Tips:

  • Work quickly once the caramel is melted, as it can harden fast as it cools.
  • Before dipping, ensure the apples are completely dry to help the caramel adhere better.
  • For additional decoration, drizzle melted chocolate over the caramel-coated apples after they have cooled.
Enjoy these homemade Gourmet Caramel Apples, decorated with your favorite toppings, for a delightful and indulgent treat!
