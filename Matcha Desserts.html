Matcha Desserts

Matcha Lava Cakes

Ingredients:

  • 4 oz (115g) white chocolate, chopped
  • ½ cup (115g) unsalted butter
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 large egg yolks
  • ¼ cup (50g) granulated sugar
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons matcha powder
  • Powdered sugar (for dusting)
  • Optional: Vanilla ice cream or whipped cream for serving

Method:

  1. Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C). Grease four ramekins or custard cups and dust with cocoa powder or flour.
  2. In a microwave-safe bowl, melt the white chocolate and butter together in short intervals, stirring until smooth. Set aside to cool slightly.
  3. In a separate bowl, whisk the eggs, egg yolks, and granulated sugar until well combined.
  4. Slowly pour the melted chocolate mixture into the egg mixture, stirring constantly.
  5. Sift in the flour and matcha powder, then gently fold until just combined.
  6. Divide the batter evenly among the prepared ramekins.
  7. Place the ramekins on a baking sheet and bake for 10-12 minutes until the edges are set but the center is slightly jiggly.
  8. Remove from the oven and let them sit for 1-2 minutes.
  9. Carefully run a knife around the edges of each cake to loosen them, then invert them onto serving plates.
  10. Dust with powdered sugar and serve immediately with vanilla ice cream or whipped cream, if desired.

Tips:

  • Ensure not to overbake the lava cakes; the center should remain slightly gooey for the lava effect.
  • Serve the cakes immediately after removing them from the oven for the best texture and molten center.

Matcha Ice Cream

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups (480ml) coconut milk (full fat)
  • ½ cup (120ml) maple syrup or agave nectar
  • 2 tablespoons matcha powder
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Method:

  1. In a blender, combine coconut milk, maple syrup or agave nectar, matcha powder, and vanilla extract.
  2. Blend until the mixture is smooth and the matcha powder is fully incorporated.
  3. Pour the mixture into an ice cream maker and churn according to the manufacturer's instructions.
  4. Once churned, transfer the ice cream to a freezer-safe container and freeze for an additional 2-3 hours to firm up.
  5. Serve the matcha ice cream scooped into bowls or cones.

Tips:

  • For a creamier texture, use full-fat coconut milk.
  • Ensure the ice cream maker's bowl is properly frozen before churning for best results.
  • If you don't have an ice cream maker, you can pour the mixture into a shallow dish, freeze for 1 hour, whisk to break up ice crystals, then freeze again until firm, repeating the process a couple of times.
