Greek -Greek Salad (Horiatiki)

Ingredients:

  • Tomatoes, cucumbers, red onions
  • Feta cheese
  • Kalamata olives
  • Olive oil and red wine vinegar
  • Dried oregano
  • Optional: green bell peppers, capers

Method:

Preparation Steps:

  • Chop tomatoes, cucumbers, and onions into chunks.

Assembly:

  • Combine chopped vegetables in a bowl, top with feta cheese and olives.
  • Drizzle olive oil and red wine vinegar, sprinkle with dried oregano.

Tips:

  • Use ripe and flavorful tomatoes for authenticity.
  • Serve chilled for a refreshing taste.
Enjoy these homemade Greek Salad (Horiatiki)
