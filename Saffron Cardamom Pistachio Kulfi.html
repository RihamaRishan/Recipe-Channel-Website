Saffron Cardamom Pistachio Kulfi

Ingredients:

  • 4 cups whole milk
  • 1/2 cup condensed milk
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cardamom
  • A pinch of saffron threads
  • 1/4 cup chopped pistachios (plus extra for garnish)

Instructions:

  1. In a heavy-bottomed pan, pour the whole milk and bring it to a boil over medium heat, stirring frequently to prevent burning.
  2. Once the milk starts boiling, reduce the heat to low and let it simmer. Stir occasionally and let the milk reduce by about half its original volume. This process might take about 45 minutes to an hour. Keep scraping the sides of the pan to incorporate the cream back into the milk.
  3. While the milk is simmering, soak the saffron threads in a tablespoon of warm milk and set it aside for a few minutes.
  4. Once the milk has reduced, add the condensed milk, sugar, ground cardamom, soaked saffron threads (along with the milk), and chopped pistachios. Mix well.
  5. Simmer the mixture for an additional 5-10 minutes, stirring continuously until it thickens slightly.
  6. Remove the pan from heat and let the mixture cool to room temperature.
  7. Pour the kulfi mixture into kulfi molds or small individual cups.
  8. Insert popsicle sticks or wooden skewers into each mold/cup. If using small cups, cover them with foil and insert a stick through the foil.
  9. Freeze the kulfi for at least 6-8 hours or overnight until fully set.

Tips:

  • To enhance the flavor, you can roast the pistachios lightly before chopping and adding them to the kulfi mixture.
  • For extra richness, you can also add a tablespoon of khoya (mawa) to the simmering milk.
Enjoy this creamy and aromatic Saffron Cardamom Pistachio Kulfi as a deliciously refreshing frozen dessert with a blend of saffron, cardamom, and pistachio flavors!
