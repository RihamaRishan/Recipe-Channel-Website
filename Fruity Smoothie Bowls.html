Fruity Smoothie Bowls

Ingredients:

  • 1 ripe banana, frozen
  • 1 cup mixed frozen berries (strawberries, blueberries, raspberries)
  • 1/2 cup yogurt (Greek yogurt, almond yogurt, or any preferred type)
  • 1/4 cup milk (dairy or non-dairy)
  • Toppings: sliced fresh fruits, granola, nuts, seeds, shredded coconut, etc.

Method:

  1. In a blender, combine the frozen banana, mixed berries, yogurt, and milk.
  2. Blend until smooth and creamy. Add more milk if needed to reach the desired consistency.
  3. Pour the smoothie into a bowl.
  4. Top the smoothie bowl with sliced fresh fruits, granola, nuts, seeds, or any preferred toppings.
  5. Enjoy your delicious and colorful fruity smoothie bowl!

Tips:

  • Customize your smoothie bowl with your favorite fruits and toppings for added texture and flavor.
  • Adjust the thickness of the smoothie by adding more or less milk.
  • Experiment with different combinations of fruits and superfood toppings for variety.
