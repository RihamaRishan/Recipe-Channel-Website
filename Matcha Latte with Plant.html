Matcha Latte with Plant

Ingredients:

  • 1 teaspoon matcha powder
  • 1 cup plant-based milk (almond, oat, soy, etc.)
  • Sweetener (optional): honey, agave, or maple syrup

Method:

  1. Heat the plant-based milk in a small saucepan until it's hot but not boiling.
  2. Place the matcha powder in a cup or bowl.
  3. Add a small amount of hot water to the matcha powder and whisk vigorously until it forms a paste, ensuring no lumps.
  4. Pour the hot milk into the matcha paste and whisk until well combined and frothy.
  5. Add sweetener if desired and stir.
  6. Serve hot and enjoy!

Tips:

  • Use a bamboo whisk for better incorporation of matcha powder and a smoother texture.
  • Adjust the amount of matcha powder and sweetener to suit your taste preferences.
