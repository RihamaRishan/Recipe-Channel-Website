Baked Brie with Jam

Ingredients:

  • 1 round of Brie cheese (about 8-12 ounces)
  • 1/4 cup jam or preserves (apricot, raspberry, fig, etc.)
  • 1/4 cup chopped nuts (optional)
  • Crackers or bread for serving

Method:

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Remove any packaging from the Brie and place it on a baking dish or oven-safe serving dish.
  3. Score the top rind of the Brie lightly with a knife.
  4. Spread the jam or preserves on top of the Brie.
  5. If desired, sprinkle chopped nuts on top of the jam.
  6. Bake the Brie for 10-12 minutes or until it becomes gooey and slightly melted.
  7. Remove from the oven and let it cool for a few minutes before serving.

Tips:

  • Use a variety of jams or preserves to find the flavor combination you prefer.
  • Serve with crackers, sliced baguette, or apple slices for a delightful sweet and savory experience.
  • Be careful not to overbake the Brie, as it might become too runny.
Enjoy your delicious Baked Brie with Jam as an appetizer or starter!
