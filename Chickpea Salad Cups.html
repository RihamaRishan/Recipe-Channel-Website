Chickpea Salad Cups

Ingredients:

  • 1 can (15 ounces) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1/4 cup diced red bell pepper
  • 1/4 cup diced cucumber
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped red onion
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Lettuce leaves or mini lettuce cups for serving

Method:

  1. In a mixing bowl, mash the chickpeas lightly with a fork or potato masher.
  2. Add diced red bell pepper, cucumber, red onion, and chopped parsley to the bowl.
  3. Drizzle olive oil and lemon juice over the mixture. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Mix everything together until well combined.
  5. Spoon the chickpea salad into lettuce cups or onto individual lettuce leaves.

Tips:

  • Adjust the ingredients according to your taste preferences. You can add additional vegetables or herbs for more flavor.
  • Let the salad sit for a little while before serving to allow the flavors to meld together.
  • If using lettuce cups, choose sturdy leaves that can hold the salad without tearing easily.
Enjoy your refreshing Chickpea Salad Cups as a starter or light appetizer!
