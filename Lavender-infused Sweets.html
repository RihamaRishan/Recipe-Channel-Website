Lavender-infused Sweets

Lavender Shortbread Cookies

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter, softened
  • ½ cup granulated sugar
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons culinary lavender buds (finely chopped or ground)
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • Optional: Extra lavender buds for decoration

Method:

  1. Preheat your oven to 325°F (160°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. In a mixing bowl, cream together the softened butter and sugar until light and fluffy.
  3. Gradually add the flour, chopped lavender buds, and salt to the butter-sugar mixture. Mix until a dough forms.
  4. Roll the dough into a ball and flatten it slightly into a disk.
  5. On a floured surface, roll out the dough to about ¼ inch thickness.
  6. Use cookie cutters to cut out shapes from the dough and place them onto the prepared baking sheet.
  7. Optional: Sprinkle extra lavender buds on top of each cookie for decoration.
  8. Bake for 12-15 minutes or until the edges of the cookies just begin to turn golden brown.
  9. Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for a few minutes before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely.

Tips:

  • Make sure to finely grind or chop the lavender buds for better distribution of flavor in the cookies.
  • Adjust the amount of lavender based on your preference for a subtle or stronger lavender flavor.

Lavender Lemon Bars

Ingredients:

For the Shortbread Crust:

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • ⅓ cup powdered sugar
  • ½ cup unsalted butter, chilled and cubed
  • 1 tablespoon culinary lavender buds (finely chopped or ground)

For the Lemon Filling:

  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 4 large eggs
  • Zest of 2 lemons
  • ½ cup freshly squeezed lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon culinary lavender buds

Method:

For the Shortbread Crust:

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Line an 8x8-inch baking pan with parchment paper, leaving an overhang on the sides.
  2. In a food processor, combine flour, powdered sugar, chopped lavender buds, and cubed butter. Pulse until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
  3. Press the mixture evenly into the bottom of the prepared baking pan.
  4. Bake for 18-20 minutes or until the edges are lightly golden. Remove from the oven and let it cool slightly.

For the Lemon Filling:

  1. In a mixing bowl, whisk together granulated sugar, flour, eggs, lemon zest, lemon juice, and lavender buds until well combined.
  2. Pour the lemon mixture over the baked crust.
  3. Return the pan to the oven and bake for an additional 20-25 minutes or until the filling is set.
  4. Allow the bars to cool completely in the pan before slicing into squares.
  5. Optional: Dust the top with powdered sugar before serving.

Tips:

  • Strain out the lavender buds from the lemon filling if you prefer a smoother texture.
  • Store these bars in the refrigerator for freshness.
These lavender-infused sweets offer a delightful and aromatic twist to classic treats, perfect for those who appreciate floral notes in their desserts!
