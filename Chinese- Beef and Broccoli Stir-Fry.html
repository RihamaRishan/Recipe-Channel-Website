Chinese - Beef and Broccoli Stir-Fry

Ingredients:

  • Sliced beef (flank steak works well)
  • Broccoli florets
  • Soy sauce
  • Oyster sauce
  • Garlic and ginger, minced
  • Cornstarch
  • Cooking oil

Method:

Preparation Steps:

  • Marinate beef slices with soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and cornstarch.

Cooking Instructions:

  • Stir-fry marinated beef in a hot pan until browned, then set aside.
  • Stir-fry broccoli until tender-crisp.
  • Return beef to the pan, add oyster sauce, and stir to combine.

Tips:

  • Cut beef against the grain for tenderness.
  • Keep the wok or pan hot for a proper stir-fry.
Enjoy your Crispy Beef and Broccoli Stir-Fry
