Churro Ice Cream Sandwiches

Ingredients:

For the Churros:

  • 1 cup water
  • 2 ½ tablespoons granulated sugar
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • Oil (for frying)
  • ½ cup granulated sugar mixed with 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon (for coating)

For the Ice Cream Filling:

  • Vanilla ice cream (or any flavor of your choice), slightly softened

Method:

For the Churros:

  1. In a saucepan, combine water, sugar, salt, and vegetable oil. Bring it to a boil.
  2. Remove the saucepan from the heat and stir in the flour until a dough forms.
  3. Heat oil in a deep pan or pot to 350°F (175°C).
  4. Spoon the churro dough into a piping bag fitted with a large star tip.
  5. Pipe 4-5 inch long strips of dough directly into the hot oil, using scissors to cut the dough.
  6. Fry until golden brown, about 2-3 minutes per side. Remove and drain on paper towels briefly.
  7. While still warm, roll the churros in the cinnamon-sugar mixture to coat evenly. Set aside to cool.

Assembly:

  1. Once the churros have cooled slightly, take a scoop of slightly softened ice cream and sandwich it between two churros.
  2. Gently press the churros together to spread the ice cream to the edges.
  3. Repeat with the remaining churros and ice cream.
  4. Optionally, roll the edges of the ice cream sandwiches in additional cinnamon-sugar mixture for extra flavor.

Tips:

  • Ensure the oil for frying is at the correct temperature to avoid churros becoming too greasy or undercooked.
  • Work quickly when assembling the ice cream sandwiches to prevent the churros from becoming too soft.
  • You can make the churros ahead of time and store them in an airtight container, but assemble the sandwiches just before serving for the best texture.
Enjoy these delightful churro ice cream sandwiches—a perfect combination of crispy, cinnamon-sugar coated churros with creamy ice cream in the middle!
