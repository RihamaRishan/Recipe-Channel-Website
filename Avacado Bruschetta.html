Avacado Bruschetta

Ingredients:

  • 1 bagcette, sliced diagonally
  • 2 ripe avocados, peeled and pitted
  • 1 large tomato, diced
  • 1/4 cup red onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tablespoons fresh basil, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Balsamic glaze for drizzling

Method:

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Place baguette slices on a baking sheet and toast in the oven for 8-10 minutes until crispy.
  2. In a bowl, mash the avocados with lime juice, salt, and pepper.
  3. In another bowl, combine diced tomato, red onion, basil, olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  4. Spread the avocado mixture onto the toasted baguette slices.
  5. Top with the tomato mixture.
  6. Drizzle with balsamic glaze before serving.

Tips:

  • Choose ripe avocados for easier mashing and better flavor.
  • Rub the toasted baguette slices with a garlic clove for added flavor before spreading the avocado mixture.
Enjoy your Avocado Bruschetta!
