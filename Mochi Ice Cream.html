Mochi Ice Cream

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup mochiko (sweet rice flour)
  • 1 ½ cups water
  • ½ cup granulated sugar
  • Cornstarch (for dusting)
  • Ice cream of your choice (scooped into balls and frozen)

Method:

  1. In a microwave-safe bowl, mix mochiko, water, and sugar until smooth.
  2. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and microwave it on high for 2 minutes.
  3. Remove the bowl from the microwave and stir the mixture.
  4. Re-cover the bowl and microwave for another 2 minutes.
  5. Stir the mixture again, re-cover, and microwave for an additional 1-2 minutes until the mixture is thick and gel-like.
  6. Let the mixture cool for a few minutes until it's cool enough to handle.

Assembling:

  1. Dust a clean work surface with cornstarch to prevent sticking.
  2. Scoop a tablespoon-sized portion of the mochi mixture onto the cornstarch-dusted surface.
  3. Flatten the mochi with your hands or a rolling pin into a small circle.
  4. Place a frozen scoop of ice cream in the center of the flattened mochi circle.
  5. Gather the edges of the mochi up and around the ice cream ball, pinching it closed at the top. Twist and seal the mochi around the ice cream ball.
  6. Repeat the process for the remaining mochi and ice cream.
  7. Place the assembled mochi ice cream on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper and freeze for at least 1-2 hours until firm.

Tips:

  • Work quickly while assembling the mochi ice cream to prevent the ice cream from melting.
  • If the mochi becomes too sticky while assembling, dust your hands with cornstarch.
  • Store the finished mochi ice cream in an airtight container in the freezer for up to a week.
Enjoy your homemade mochi ice cream!
