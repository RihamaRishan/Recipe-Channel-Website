French madelines

Ingredients:

  • 2/3 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 2 large eggs, at room temperature
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Zest of 1 lemon or orange (optional)
  • 9 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted and cooled, plus extra for greasing the molds
  • Powdered sugar for dusting (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Brush the madeleine molds with melted butter and lightly dust with flour, tapping out any excess. Place the pan in the refrigerator to chill while you prepare the batter.
  2. In a bowl, sift together the flour, baking powder, and salt. Set aside.
  3. In another bowl, whisk together the eggs, granulated sugar, and vanilla extract until pale and slightly thickened. If using citrus zest, add it to the mixture.
  4. Gradually fold the dry ingredients into the egg mixture, being careful not to overmix. Mix until just combined.
  5. Gently fold in the melted butter until incorporated into the batter. Let the batter rest in the refrigerator for about 30 minutes.
  6. Spoon the batter into the chilled madeleine molds, filling each about 3/4 full.
  7. Bake in the preheated oven for 10-12 minutes or until the edges are golden and the centers spring back when lightly touched.
  8. Remove the madeleines from the oven and let them cool in the pan for a few minutes before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely.

Tips:

  • For the characteristic "hump," chill the batter and the filled molds before baking.
  • Madeleines are best enjoyed fresh but can be stored in an airtight container for a day or two.
  • Dust the madeleines with powdered sugar before serving for an elegant touch.
Enjoy these classic French Madeleines with a cup of tea or coffee for a delightful treat!
