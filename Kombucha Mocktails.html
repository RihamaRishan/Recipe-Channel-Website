Kombucha Mocktails

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup kombucha (plain or flavored)
  • 1/2 cup sparkling water or soda
  • Fresh fruits (berries, citrus slices, etc.)
  • Fresh herbs (mint, basil, etc.)
  • Ice cubes

Method:

  1. In a glass, combine kombucha and sparkling water.
  2. Add ice cubes and stir gently to mix.
  3. Garnish with fresh fruits and herbs.
  4. Serve chilled and enjoy!

Tips:

  • Experiment with different kombucha flavors and fruit/herb combinations for varying tastes.
