Crispy Cauliflower Bites

Ingredients:

  • 1 medium cauliflower head, cut into florets
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 cup buffalo sauce
  • Cooking oil for frying

Method:

  1. In a bowl, mix breadcrumbs, flour, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  2. Dip cauliflower florets in water, then coat with the breadcrumb mixture.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and fry cauliflower until golden brown and crispy.
  4. Toss fried cauliflower in buffalo sauce until coated.

Tips:

  • For a healthier option, bake the coated cauliflower in the oven instead of frying.
  • Adjust the level of spice in the buffalo sauce according to your preference.
Enjoy your Crispy Cauliflower Bites!
