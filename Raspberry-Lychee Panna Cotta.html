Raspberry-Lychee Panna Cotta

Ingredients:

  • 1 can (approx. 15 oz) lychees in syrup
  • 1 cup fresh or frozen raspberries
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar (adjust to taste)
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 packet (about 2 1/4 teaspoons) unflavored gelatin
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions:

  1. Drain the lychees and reserve the syrup. Remove the seeds from the lychees and set them aside.
  2. In a saucepan, combine the raspberries, lychee syrup, and sugar. Cook over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until the raspberries break down and the mixture thickens slightly. Remove from heat and strain the mixture through a fine-mesh sieve to remove seeds and solids. Allow it to cool.
  3. In a separate saucepan, pour the milk and sprinkle the gelatin over the surface. Let it sit for about 5 minutes to bloom.
  4. Heat the milk and gelatin mixture over low heat, stirring continuously until the gelatin dissolves completely. Remove from heat and let it cool slightly.
  5. In a bowl, mix the heavy cream and vanilla extract. Add the cooled gelatin-milk mixture and whisk until well combined.
  6. Divide the raspberry-lychee puree evenly among serving glasses or molds, filling each about 1/3 full.
  7. Carefully pour the cream mixture over the puree in each glass, filling to the top.
  8. Refrigerate the panna cotta for at least 4 hours or until set.

Tips:

  • Adjust the sweetness by altering the amount of sugar used in the raspberry-lychee puree.
  • To easily remove the panna cotta from molds, briefly dip the bottom of the mold in warm water before unmolding onto serving plates.
Enjoy this elegant and flavorful Raspberry-Lychee Panna Cotta as a stunning dessert option that combines the sweetness of lychee with the tartness of raspberries!
