Spring Rolls

Ingredients:

  • 8-10 spring roll wrappers
  • 2 cups shredded cabbage or coleslaw mix
  • 1 cup julienned carrots
  • 1 cup thinly sliced bell peppers (any color)
  • 1/2 cup thinly sliced cucumber
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro or mint leaves
  • 8-10 cooked shrimp, optional (if non-vegetarian)
  • Rice vermicelli noodles, cooked according to package instructions
  • Hoisin sauce or sweet chili sauce for dipping
  • Oil for frying or water for fresh spring rolls

Method:

  1. Prepare all the vegetables and lay them out on a clean, damp kitchen towel or paper towel to remove excess moisture.
  2. Dip a spring roll wrapper into a bowl of warm water for a few seconds until it softens. Lay it flat on a clean surface.
  3. Place a small amount of each vegetable and a shrimp (if using) in the center of the wrapper, leaving space on the sides.
  4. Fold the sides of the wrapper over the filling and then roll it up tightly, tucking in the sides as you roll.
  5. Repeat with the remaining wrappers and filling.
  6. If frying, heat oil in a pan and fry the spring rolls until golden brown and crispy. If not frying, serve them as fresh spring rolls.
  7. Serve with hoisin sauce or sweet chili sauce for dipping.

Tips:

  • Keep the spring roll wrappers covered with a damp towel while assembling to prevent them from drying out.
  • Do not overfill the spring rolls to avoid tearing the wrappers.
  • For a healthier option, you can bake the spring rolls in the oven instead of frying them.
Enjoy your delicious Vegetable Spring Rolls as a starter or appetizer!
