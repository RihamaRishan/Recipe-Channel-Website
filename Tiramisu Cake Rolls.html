Tiramisu Cake Rolls

Ingredients:

For the Cake:

  • 4 large eggs, separated
  • ¾ cup (150g) granulated sugar, divided
  • ⅓ cup (40g) cake flour
  • ¼ cup (30g) unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • ¼ teaspoon salt

For the Filling:

  • 1 cup (240ml) heavy cream
  • 8 oz (225g) mascarpone cheese, softened
  • ⅓ cup (40g) powdered sugar
  • 2 tablespoons coffee liqueur (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons brewed espresso or strong coffee, cooled
  • Cocoa powder, for dusting

Method:

For the Cake:

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a 10x15-inch jelly roll pan with parchment paper, leaving an overhang on the long sides. Lightly grease the parchment paper.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, beat egg yolks and ½ cup sugar until pale and thick.
  3. In another bowl, sift together the cake flour, cocoa powder, baking powder, and salt.
  4. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the egg yolk mixture, mixing until just combined.
  5. In a separate clean bowl, beat the egg whites until foamy. Gradually add the remaining ¼ cup of sugar and continue beating until stiff peaks form.
  6. Gently fold the egg whites into the batter until just incorporated.
  7. Spread the batter evenly onto the prepared pan and bake for 12-15 minutes until the cake springs back when touched lightly.
  8. While the cake is baking, lay a clean kitchen towel on the counter and dust it with cocoa powder.
  9. Once the cake is baked, immediately invert it onto the prepared towel. Remove the parchment paper carefully.
  10. Roll the cake, starting from the shorter end, using the towel. Let it cool completely while rolled.

For the Filling:

  1. In a mixing bowl, beat the heavy cream until stiff peaks form.
  2. In another bowl, beat the mascarpone cheese and powdered sugar until smooth. Add coffee liqueur (if using) and brewed espresso, mixing until combined.
  3. Gently fold the whipped cream into the mascarpone mixture until smooth.

Assembling:

  1. Carefully unroll the cooled cake and spread the filling evenly over the cake.
  2. Roll the cake back up, without the towel, and place it seam-side down on a serving platter.
  3. Dust the top of the cake with cocoa powder.
  4. Refrigerate the cake for at least 2 hours before slicing and serving.

Tips:

  • Ensure the cake is completely cooled before spreading the filling to prevent it from melting and making the roll soggy.
  • Rolling the cake while warm helps prevent cracking when assembling it later.
  • Use a sharp serrated knife to slice the cake roll for clean and neat servings.
