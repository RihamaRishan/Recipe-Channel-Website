Mexican - Chicken Enchiladas

Ingredients:

  • Cooked shredded chicken
  • Corn tortillas
  • Enchilada sauce
  • Shredded cheese
  • Chopped onions and cilantro (optional)
  • Sour cream and sliced avocado (for serving)

Method:

Preparation Steps:

  • Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
  • Warm tortillas in a skillet or microwave to make them pliable.

Cooking Instructions:*

  • Dip each tortilla in enchilada sauce, fill with shredded chicken, and roll it up.
  • Place the filled tortillas seam side down in a baking dish.
  • Pour remaining enchilada sauce over the top, sprinkle with shredded cheese, onions, and cilantro.
  • Bake for 20-25 minutes until cheese is bubbly.

Tips:

  • Use a flavorful enchilada sauce for enhanced taste.
  • Customize by adding different fillings like beans or vegetables.
