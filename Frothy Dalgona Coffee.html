Frothy Dalgona Coffee

Ingredients:

  • 2 tablespoons instant coffee
  • 2 tablespoons sugar (adjust to taste)
  • 2 tablespoons hot water
  • Milk (dairy or non-dairy)
  • Ice cubes (if making an iced version)

Method:

  1. In a mixing bowl, combine instant coffee, sugar, and hot water in a 1:1:1 ratio.
  2. Using a hand mixer, stand mixer, or a whisk, beat the mixture vigorously until it becomes thick, creamy, and forms stiff peaks. This may take about 5-10 minutes depending on the method used.
  3. In a glass, pour milk over ice cubes if making an iced coffee.
  4. Top the milk with the whipped coffee mixture.
  5. Gently stir the whipped coffee into the milk before drinking.
  6. Enjoy your frothy Dalgona coffee!

Tips:

  • Adjust the amount of sugar and instant coffee to suit your taste preferences.
  • For a richer flavor, use warm or cold milk instead of hot milk.
  • The key to achieving the frothy texture is to whip the coffee mixture vigorously until it thickens and forms peaks.
Enjoy your Frothy Dalgona Coffee
