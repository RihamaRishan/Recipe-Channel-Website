Caprese Skewers

Ingredients:

  • Cherry or grape tomatoes
  • Fresh mozzarella cheese, cut into cubes or balls
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Balsamic glaze
  • Wooden skewers

Method:

  1. Rinse and dry the cherry tomatoes and basil leaves.
  2. Thread one cherry tomato, one cube of mozzarella, and one basil leaf onto each wooden skewer. Repeat until the skewer is filled.
  3. Arrange the skewers on a serving platter.
  4. Drizzle balsamic glaze over the skewers just before serving.

Tips:

  • Use fresh, high-quality mozzarella for the best flavor.
  • Soak the wooden skewers in water for about 30 minutes before assembling to prevent burning while cooking.
  • Alternate the order of ingredients for a visually appealing presentation.
Enjoy your delightful Caprese Skewers!
