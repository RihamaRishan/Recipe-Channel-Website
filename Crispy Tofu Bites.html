Crispy Tofu Bites

Ingredients:

  • 1 block firm tofu, pressed and cut into bite-sized cubes
  • 1/2 cup cornstarch
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Cooking oil for frying
  • Sauce of your choice (teriyaki, sweet chili, etc.) for coating

Method:

  1. Press the tofu to remove excess moisture by wrapping it in paper towels and placing a weight on top for about 20-30 minutes.
  2. In a bowl, mix cornstarch, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  3. Toss the tofu cubes in the cornstarch mixture until evenly coated.
  4. Heat oil in a pan over medium-high heat.
  5. Fry the tofu cubes in batches for about 4-5 minutes, turning occasionally until all sides are golden and crispy. Remove and drain excess oil on paper towels.
  6. Coat the crispy tofu cubes with your preferred sauce while they're still warm.

Tips:

  • Use extra firm tofu to achieve a crispier texture.
  • Ensure the oil is hot before adding the tofu to prevent it from becoming too oily.
  • Pat the tofu dry before coating it to ensure the cornstarch mixture sticks well.
Enjoy your Crispy Tofu Bites as a delightful starter or snack!
