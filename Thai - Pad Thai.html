Thai - Pad Thai

Ingredients:

  • Rice noodles
  • Shrimp or tofu
  • Bean sprouts
  • Scallions (green onions)
  • Eggs
  • Peanuts
  • Tamarind paste, fish sauce, sugar, and chili flakes

Method:

Preparation Steps:

  • Soak rice noodles in hot water until softened.

Cooking Instructions:

  • Sauté shrimp/tofu, then push to one side and scramble eggs in the same pan.
  • Add noodles, bean sprouts, scallions, and the sauce made of tamarind paste, fish sauce, sugar, and chili flakes.
  • Toss everything together until heated through.

Tips:

  • Adjust the sweetness and spice levels according to taste.
  • Garnish with crushed peanuts and lime wedges.
Enjoy your delicious Pad Thai
