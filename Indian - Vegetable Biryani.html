Indian - Vegetable Biryani

Ingredients:

  • Basmati rice
  • Assorted vegetables (like carrots, peas, potatoes)
  • Yogurt and spices (cumin, coriander, turmeric)
  • Saffron threads
  • Fried onions
  • Ghee or vegetable oil

Method:

  1. Preparation Steps:

    2. Parboil rice and marinate vegetables in yogurt and spices.

  2. Cooking Instructions:

  3. Layer rice and vegetables in a pot, alternating with fried onions and saffron-infused milk.
  4. Cover and cook until rice is fully cooked and aromatic.

Tips:

  • Soak saffron threads in warm milk for a richer flavor and color.
  • Use a heavy-bottomed pot for even cooking.
Feel free to experiment and adjust these recipes to suit your taste preferences and dietary needs!
