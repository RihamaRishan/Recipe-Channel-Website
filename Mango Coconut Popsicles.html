Mango Coconut Popsicles

Ingredients:

  • 2 ripe mangoes, peeled and diced
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 2-3 tablespoons honey or sugar (adjust to taste)
  • 1 teaspoon lime juice (optional)

Instructions:

  1. In a blender or food processor, puree the diced mangoes until smooth.
  2. Add coconut milk, honey or sugar, and lime juice (if using) to the mango puree. Blend until well combined and smooth.
  3. Taste the mixture and adjust sweetness according to your preference by adding more honey or sugar if needed.
  4. Pour the mixture into popsicle molds, leaving a little space at the top for expansion.
  5. Insert popsicle sticks or handles into each mold.
  6. Freeze the popsicles for at least 4-6 hours, or until completely frozen.

Tips:

  • For a textured variation, you can add small mango chunks into the mixture before pouring into molds.
  • To easily release the popsicles from the molds, briefly run the molds under warm water.
Enjoy these delicious and tropical Mango Coconut Popsicles as a delightful and healthy fruit-based dessert!
