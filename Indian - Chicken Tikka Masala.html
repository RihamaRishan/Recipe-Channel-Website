Indian - Chicken Tikka Masala

Ingredients:

  • Chicken breast, diced
  • Yogurt
  • Garam masala
  • Tomato puree
  • Heavy cream
  • Garlic, ginger, and onion paste
  • Vegetable oil
  • Fresh cilantro (coriander) for garnish

Method:

Preparation Steps:

  • Marinate chicken in yogurt and garam masala for at least 1 hour.

Cooking Instructions:

  • Sear marinated chicken in a pan until browned, then set aside.
  • In the same pan, sauté garlic-ginger-onion paste, add tomato puree, and cook until fragrant.
  • Add chicken back to the pan, simmer, and stir in heavy cream until heated through.
  • Garnish with fresh cilantro before serving.

Tips:

  • Adjust spice levels by varying the amount of garam masala.
  • Tenderize chicken by marinating longer for a more flavorful dish.
Enjoy your Chicken Tikka Masala
