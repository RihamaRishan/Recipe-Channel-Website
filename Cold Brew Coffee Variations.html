Cold Brew Coffee Variations

Ingredients:

  • Coarsely ground coffee beans
  • Water
  • Optional flavorings: vanilla extract, cinnamon sticks, etc.
  • Milk or cream (optional)

Method:

  1. Combine coarsely ground coffee beans and water in a jar or French press using a ratio of 1:4 (coffee to water).
  2. Stir to ensure all the grounds are wet.
  3. Cover and refrigerate for 12-24 hours.
  4. Strain the coffee concentrate using a fine-mesh sieve or a coffee filter.
  5. Serve over ice with milk or cream and any desired flavorings.
  6. Enjoy your smooth and flavorful cold brew coffee!

Tips:

  • Experiment with different brewing times and coffee-to-water ratios for varying strengths and flavors.
  • Store the cold brew concentrate in the refrigerator for up to a week.
