Turemic Golden Milk

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup milk (dairy or non-dairy)
  • 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • A pinch of black pepper
  • Sweetener (optional): honey, maple syrup, or dates

Method:

  1. In a small saucepan, heat the milk over medium heat.
  2. Add turmeric, ginger, cinnamon, and a pinch of black pepper to the milk.
  3. Whisk the mixture continuously until it's hot but not boiling.
  4. Remove from heat, add sweetener if desired, and stir well.
  5. Strain the mixture through a fine-mesh sieve into a cup.
  6. Serve warm and enjoy!

Tips:

  • Black pepper enhances the absorption of curcumin (the active ingredient in turmeric), so include a pinch for better health benefits.
  • Adjust the spices and sweetness according to your taste.
Enjoy your Turemic Golden Milk
